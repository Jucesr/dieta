# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Ingredients can store protein, carbs, fat, and sugar per 100 g, plus how many grams each unit weighs. Calories are calculated from protein, carbs, and fat.
- Sides are ingredients on the meal, up to two, kept separate from the meal’s ingredient list. The sides catalog is removed.
- Each day can optionally include a second breakfast, lunch, or dinner.
- The plan can show a nutrition table for the selected day, with protein, carbs, and fat per meal and a daily total.
- Meal times chosen in settings, including snack, are saved and restored after refresh.
